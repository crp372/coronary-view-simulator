# TotalSegmentator: written permission

**Granted** by Dr Jakob Wasserthal (TotalSegmentator, Universitätsspital Basel), by email, in reply to the request in [totalsegmentator-permission-email.md](totalsegmentator-permission-email.md). Recorded on 2026-11-05. Keep the original email in your mailbox as well: it's the written permission clause 3.4 of the licence asks for.

## What was asked

To publish, free and purely educational, with no ads, payments or sponsorship:
- Surface meshes of the chambers, myocardium, aorta and pulmonary artery, made from the `heartchambers_highres` segmentations of ImageCAS scans 814 and 905.
- Data derived from the masks: the guide-catheter path, coronary side branches kept out of the blood pool, and the heart's axis for the modelled cardiac motion.
- On a free static website (e.g. GitHub Pages), with its source in a public repository. The repository holds only the derived meshes and data, not the TotalSegmentator software, the model weights or the raw masks.
- Attribution: cite doi.org/10.1148/ryai.230024, and credit Dr Wasserthal, the Department of Radiology and Universitätsspital Basel; send him the link once live.

## Scope

The permission covers what was asked above: scans 814 and 905, those derived files, that website and repository. A new heart prepared with `heartchambers_highres`, or a different kind of use, would need asking again. The other conditions still apply: credit as promised, and send him the link once it's live.
